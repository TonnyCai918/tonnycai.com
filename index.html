01  About Me

A river town, and the habit of looking things up

I was born in Yichang, a mountain city in central China. The ranges stand at its back and the Yangtze cuts it in two, and the people it makes are shaped by both: the mountains give them the footing for a steep road, and the river the patience not to hurry on it. I grew up crossing between the city and the countryside. When someone asks where I am from, what surfaces first is the bright commotion of that river town, and a half-second later the green, unripe smell of hill water, as if my shoes were walking a paved street while still caked with country mud.

02  Research

Working papers

October 2026 · draft available

Shells Without Substance: What Happens When a Tax Haven Closes

A stacked event study around the 2018 dismantling of a border-county tax exemption, covering 183 listed parents and 1,881 haven subsidiary-years. Exposed parents become 14.6 to 22.1 percentage points less likely to report positive pre-tax profit, while revenue and taxes actually paid do not move. Havens that require real operations show an effect of zero.

November 2026 · draft available

Fifty Minus One: How a Statutory Partner Cap Built a 59,000-Entity Pyramid

59,061 limited partnerships registered by one operation in two founding years. Of 69 inter-tier capital ratios, 64 fall between 48 and 49, and none exceeds 49. The maximum is 48.99: the statutory cap of fifty partners, minus the one general partner every partnership must have.

October 2026 · draft available

Targeting Yesterday: Industrial Zones and the Selection of Declining Industries

Chinese development zones announce the industries they intend to attract. Matching 2,405 of them to a county-by-industry registration panel, registration does shift toward the declared industry. The industries declared, however, are ones already contracting nationally, and had been for years before the announcement.

November 2026 · draft available

A Screen for Manufactured Entry: Registered Capital Roundness

A capital figure a founder chooses is round. One produced by dividing a parent's capital among a fixed number of seats is a quotient, and division by anything but a power of ten destroys roundness. The screen is a single inequality, run across 81,482,453 non-individual registration records.
